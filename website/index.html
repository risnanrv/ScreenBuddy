A calm reminder to step away from your screen.

ScreenBuddy runs silently in your system tray, offering periodic fullscreen break reminders to rest your eyes, fix your posture, and recharge.

Download for Windows v1.0.0 View on GitHub

Requires Windows 10/11 (64-bit) • 100% Offline • Open Source

How ScreenBuddy Works

01

Focus & Work

Work uninterrupted for your configured focus duration. ScreenBuddy stays out of your way in the system tray.

02

Gentle Break

When focus time expires, a calm fullscreen overlay appears across all displays with a relaxing rest message.

03

Resume Rested

Once your break finishes, the overlay automatically closes and a fresh work session begins.

Designed for Focus & Peace of Mind

Custom Work & Break Intervals

Configure work sessions from 1 to 120 minutes and rest breaks from 1 to 60 minutes.

Snooze & Skip Control

Presenting or in a critical call? Postpone breaks for 1–5 minutes or skip to the next session effortlessly.

Minimize Break

Hide the fullscreen screen overlay while taking your break in the background.

Emergency Escape (ESC)

Never get trapped. Hold Escape for 3 seconds to immediately pause ScreenBuddy and return to your desktop.

Multi-Monitor Support

Extends seamlessly across all connected displays with synchronized countdowns.

100% Offline & Private

No network calls, no telemetry, no analytics, no accounts. Your data never leaves your computer.

Zero Telemetry. Zero Tracking.

ScreenBuddy believes software should respect your privacy. ScreenBuddy makes zero network connections, does not collect analytics or crash reports, and requires no user account or login.
