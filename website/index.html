How ScreenBuddy Works

01

Choose Your Focus Time

Configure your work duration. ScreenBuddy stays accessible from your desktop and system tray.

02

Work Normally

Focus on your work while ScreenBuddy silently keeps track of your screen time.

03

ScreenBuddy Reminds You

Why ScreenBuddy

Fullscreen Break Reminders

Gentle fullscreen screens across all monitors to help you disconnect.

Custom Work & Break Durations

Configure focus sessions and rest intervals directly from the main window.

Snooze When Timing Isn't Right

In a call or deep focus? Snooze breaks for 1 to 5 minutes effortlessly.

Pause During Meetings

Pause screen timers whenever you need extended uninterrupted focus.

Single Control Center

A central desktop window controls all session states, backed by quiet tray operation.

Zero Telemetry

100% offline. No accounts, no analytics, no cloud dependency.

Privacy First

ScreenBuddy doesn't need your data. It makes zero network calls, collects zero telemetry, and stores all settings locally on your computer.
